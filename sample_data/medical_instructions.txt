PATIENT PRE-OPERATIVE PREPARATION PROTOCOL

It is recommended that patients undergoing ambulatory surgical interventions abstain from oral alimentary ingestion, including liquid beverages, for a duration of no less than eight hours prior to anesthesiologic administration. Failure to comply with pre-procedure fasting guidelines may precipitate pulmonary aspiration of gastric contents, resulting in severe respiratory complications.

Patients should take their prescribed antihypertensive pharmacotherapy with a minimal quantity of water upon awakening. If diabetic medication is currently prescribed, dosage adjustment should be discussed with the attending physician immediately.
